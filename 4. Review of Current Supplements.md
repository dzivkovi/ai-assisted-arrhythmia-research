The review provided in the sources highlights which components of your current stack are particularly **beneficial** for your situation and discusses potential considerations:

*   **Coenzyme Q10 (CoQ10):** This is described as supporting heart muscle energy and an antioxidant. The sources state that CoQ10 **may help reduce arrhythmia frequency**. It is considered particularly useful if your "Crestor replacement" is red yeast rice, as it can counteract potential CoQ10 depletion. CoQ10 is very well tolerated and the sources recommend **you should definitely keep taking this**.
*   **Moderate Omega-3 Fish Oil:** This reduces inflammation and supports overall heart health. The sources note that **moderate doses may have an antiarrhythmic effect**. A dose of **1g per day of combined EPA/DHA is a common, safe dose** for heart benefits. You are taking this, and it likely helps calm inflammation. The sources caution that **high doses (>4g/day) could increase the risk of atrial fibrillation** in some individuals. You should **continue taking it** at a moderate dose and **avoid mega-doses** unless directed by a physician.
*   **PS Brain Blend (Ashwagandha, Bacopa, Phosphatidylserine):** The sources explicitly state these are **likely helping to reduce stress-induced arrhythmias**. This is because they calm the nervous system and blunt cortisol response. Ashwagandha improves heart rate variability, Bacopa might slightly slow an elevated heart rate, and Phosphatidylserine blunts cortisol. The sources recommend **continuing these components**. Bacopa can cause slight bradycardia in some, so monitor if your resting heart rate is very low and you feel lightheaded, but for stress-induced palpitations, its calming effect is likely helpful. Ashwagandha can slightly raise thyroid hormones, so periodic check-ups might be wise.
*   **Tart Cherry:** This supplement is noted for its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties and may improve sleep due to natural melatonin content. It is **not known to cause arrhythmias**. By improving sleep quality and reducing inflammation, it could **indirectly help reduce palpitations**. You can continue it, especially if it aids sleep or recovery.
*   **Garlic & Curcumin:** These are described as heart-healthy, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant. They may ease cardiac workload or reduce triggers from inflammation/oxidative stress. You should **continue taking them**.

The sources also review the components of your stack that are considered neutral for arrhythmia but generally beneficial for health:

*   **"Crestor replacement":** This is likely a natural cholesterol-lowering agent (like red yeast rice). It's generally **positive for cardiovascular health** in the long run and **not known to cause arrhythmias**. You can **continue this** for cardiovascular prevention.

The review also highlights potential **cumulative effects** when combining supplements from your current stack:

*   **Cumulative Mild Blood Thinning:** Your existing Garlic, Fish Oil, and Curcumin all have mild blood-thinning properties. The sources note this combination **could slightly increase bleeding time**. This is **usually not dangerous for most people**, but you should **be aware of it** and perhaps **pause these supplements or inform doctors before any surgical procedures or dental extractions**.

Combining multiple such supplements could sometimes lead to **blood pressure that is too low, especially when standing up (orthostatic hypotension)**, causing dizziness or excessive fatigue. While this effect might be beneficial if stress raises your blood pressure, you should **be mindful of and monitor this**. If symptoms occur, you might need to **adjust dosages or timing**.
